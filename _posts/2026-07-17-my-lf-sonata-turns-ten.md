My 2017 Hyundai LF Sonata e-VGT is now in its tenth year — and it is still my first and only vehicle to date.

It has been much more than a personal car, though. This is the very vehicle from which the [X-CANIDS dataset](https://ieee-dataport.org/open-access/x-canids-dataset-vehicle-signal-dataset) was collected — the in-vehicle CAN dataset we released alongside the [X-CANIDS paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVT.2023.3327275) in IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology. The dataset has since become well known in the automotive security community and continues to be cited by researchers around the world.

Ten years in, it still runs fine — and yes, it is still shiny. ;-)
