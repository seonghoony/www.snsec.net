A collaborative paper, "**J1939DB-IDS: SAE J1939 Dual-Branch Intrusion Detection System against Novel Attacks**", has been accepted for publication in **IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management (TNSM)**. The work proposes a dual-branch intrusion detection system for the **SAE J1939** in-vehicle network of heavy-duty and commercial vehicles, with an emphasis on detecting previously unseen attacks. Congratulations to the authors on the acceptance.
