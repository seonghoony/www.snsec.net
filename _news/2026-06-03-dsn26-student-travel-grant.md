[Chaeri Jung](/people/#chaeri-jung) and [Chaeyoung Lee](/people/#chaeyoung-lee) have each been awarded a Student Travel Grant for IEEE/IFIP DSN’26, supporting their travel to present "DRIFT: Drift-Resilient Invariant-Feature Transformer for DGA Detection" at the conference in Charlotte, North Carolina. The grants were awarded by the DSN’26 Student Travel Grant Committee, co-chaired by Meera Sridhar and Domenico Cotroneo. Congratulations to both!
