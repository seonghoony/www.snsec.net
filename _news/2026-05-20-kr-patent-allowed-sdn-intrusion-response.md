Our patent, “Method for SDN-based Intrusion Response or Prevention for In-vehicle Network and System Using the Same” (KR App. No. 10-2020-0095518), covering key technical concepts reflected in the international standard [ITU-T X.1377, “Guidelines for an intrusion prevention system for connected vehicles”](https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-X.1377/en), has received a decision to grant in Korea.
