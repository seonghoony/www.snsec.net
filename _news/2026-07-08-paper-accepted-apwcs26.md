A paper by **Nayoung Kim (김나영)** and **Minjeong Kim (김민정)**, "**NPP-ITA: Normal-Prototype Prior for Unsupervised Video Anomaly Detection with Inference-Time Adaptation**", has been accepted for presentation at the **IEEE VTS Asia Pacific Wireless Communications Symposium (APWCS) 2026**, to be held in Seoul, Korea in August 2026. Nayoung and Minjeong are undergraduate students whose graduation research was supervised by Prof. Seonghoon Jeong. The paper will appear in the IEEE APWCS 2026 proceedings and be submitted to IEEE Xplore. Congratulations!
